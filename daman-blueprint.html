The Ultimate Daman Family Itinerary: A Relaxing Long Weekend Guide

A Complete Day-wise Guide for Indian Families with Kids, Parents & Vegetarians

Planning a quick weekend escape from Mumbai, Pune, or Gujarat that doesn't feel like a logistical nightmare? Daman is often dismissed as a crowded party hub, but when planned right, it is one of the easiest, most relaxing multi-generational getaways on the western coast.

This itinerary is designed specifically for families traveling with kids and senior parents who want a seamless balance of relaxation, safe beach time, light sightseeing, and reliable pure-vegetarian dining.

Before You Start: The Real Talk About Daman

  • Forget the Party Stereotype: If you stay near crowded liquor zones, you will hate it. If you anchor your stay at a premium beachfront property or a luxury resort, Daman feels peaceful, organized, and remarkably upscale.
  • The Infrastructure is Exceptional: Unlike many coastal getaways plagued by broken village roads, Daman features clean, European-style wide roads with virtually zero potholes. The drive is exceptionally gentle on elderly parents and toddlers.
  • Afternoon Heat is Real: Coastal humidity peaks between 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM. Do not schedule heavy sightseeing in the afternoon. Build your days around a mandatory resort pool and recharge break.

Best Hotels & Resorts for Families

For a multi-generational trip, your accommodation must have expansive pool access, spacious rooms, and accommodating kitchens:

  • The Deltin (5-Star Luxury): The top full-service property in the region, featuring massive winding pools and spacious family setups.
  • The Fern Seaside Luxurious Tent Resort (4-Star Luxury): A truly unique luxury experience right by the sea. They offer high-end, air-conditioned "glamping" tents with private sit-out areas. The property is lush, incredibly well-maintained, and features a pristine swimming pool.

VegStroller Tip: Their culinary team is highly rated, and they are exceptionally pet-friendly if you are traveling with a furry friend!

  • The Gold Beach Resort (4-Star Beachfront): Direct access to the Devka promenade with sea-facing balconies.
  • Nest Villa (Jampore Beach): Ideal for larger family groups seeking privacy, offering exclusive villas with private plunge pools.
  • Praveg Beach Resort (Near Jampore): Premium cottage-style living steps away from the lighthouse.
  • Mirasol Resort (Family Entertainment): Features peddle boating, a water park, and indoor game rooms for kids.

Day-wise Family Daman Itinerary

Day 1: Smooth Check-in, Bird Aviary, Go-Karting & Flat Seabed Sunsets

  • 2:00 PM – Arrival & Check-In: Arrive, settle in, unpack, and let everyone decompress after the drive.
  • 4:00 PM – Daman Bird Aviary (1 Hour): A gentle, shaded, beautifully landscaped walk suitable for senior citizens. Kids get an up-close look at exotic birds without demanding physical exertion.
  • 5:00 PM – Go-Karting (30–45 Mins): Located right near the aviary zone, this adds an easy adrenaline boost for older kids and teens before heading to the coast.
  • 5:30 PM – Jampore Beach at Sunset: Unlike rocky or steep shores, Jampore has a remarkably flat seabed. The water stays shallow for hundreds of meters, allowing children to play safely while adults relax along the promenade.
  • 7:30 PM – Freshen Up & Dinner:
    • Option A (Lively Vibe): Head to Daman Culture near Jampore Beach for weekend music and open-air energy.
    • Option B (Quiet & Relaxed): Head to Nest Beacon right next door for a calmer atmosphere. (Note: Multi-cuisine, but their vegetarian food is well-prepared, and fresh).

Family Tip for Day 1: Carry a dry bag with extra towels and a change of clothes in the car for Jampore Beach so the kids aren't sitting wet on the drive back to the hotel.

Day 2: Portuguese Heritage, Pool Downtime, Devka Beach & Night Markets

  • 10:00 AM – Fort Area & Portuguese Quarter (1.5 to 2 Hours):
    • Moti Daman Fort & The Lighthouse: Easy ramp walks and panoramic coastal views.
    • 🏛️ Asia's Oldest Municipality: While walking through the fort complex, you will pass the Daman Municipal Council building. Established during the Portuguese era, it holds the title of being the oldest municipality in Asia.
    • The Bhool Bhulaiya (Labyrinth): An interactive maze kids enjoy exploring.
    • Church of Our Lady of the Rosary: 400-year-old Portuguese carved woodwork in a quiet, contemplative atmosphere.
    • Portuguese Street Walk: Stroll the heritage quarter past pastel-colored colonial facades for family photographs.
    • 🚨 Must-Try Local Treat: Look for the local vendor selling artisanal Gud (Jaggery) Lassi. It is refreshing, digestive, and completely unique to this pocket. You can also visit Papyrus Cafe near the municipality.
  • 1:00 PM – Pure-Veg Lunch & Resort Pool Break: Have lunch at Veera’s Pure Veg or return to your resort. Spend 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM resting, taking a dip in the pool, or letting grandparents rest during peak sun.
  • 4:30 PM – Devka Beach, Promenade & Gardens: Head to Devka Beach as temperatures drop. Opposite the beach is an amusement garden featuring a toy train that younger kids love.
  • 6:30 PM – Illuminated Coastal Walk: After dusk, the Devka promenade lights up with paved walkways, sea breezes, and clean sitting plazas.
  • 7:30 PM – Dinner & Music:
    • Cidade de Daman: Features an active dance floor, live DJ, and high-energy dining right on the shore.
    • Waves Club: Adjacent option offering a slightly more relaxed lounge and dining layout.
  • 9:00 PM – Devka Night Market: Take a slow walk through the night stalls directly along the strip for souvenirs, handicrafts, and beachside snacks.

Absolute Must-Know Tips for Day 2:

🚨 Wear Slip-On Shoes: You will transition between churches, forts, and sandy paths; avoid lace-up shoes for kids and elderly parents.

🚨 Skip Devka for Swimming: Devka Beach is rocky during low tide. Save swimming and water play for Jampore Beach or your resort pool; treat Devka strictly as a sunset, promenade, and garden destination.

Day 3: Slow Morning, Resort Breakfast & Departure

  • 9:00 AM: Enjoy an unhurried buffet breakfast.
  • 10:30 AM: Pack up, check out, and start the highway drive back before midday traffic sets in.

Daman Family Itinerary — At A Glance

Pure-Vegetarian & Family Dining Guide

Finding reliable food in a coastal town known for seafood can cause anxiety. Here is where to go:

  1. Veera’s Pure Veg: The most dependable 100% vegetarian restaurant in Daman for North Indian, Punjabi, and tandoor dishes. High turnover ensures fresh food and clean prep.
  2. Kathiawadi Thali Outlets (Daman-Vapi Road): For wholesome regional meals, several traditional dining halls serve unlimited Gujarati thalis (bajra roti, ringna no olo, sev tameta) with high hygiene standards.
  3. The Executive Chef Route: At 4- and 5-star properties, inform the kitchen team upon arrival if you avoid onion and garlic (Jain options) or require dedicated preparation areas. Coastal luxury hotels routinely accommodate custom meal plans when requested in advance.

Getting Around: Rentals & Local Transport

  • Two-Wheeler & Bicycle Rentals: You can rent bicycles for ₹50–₹100 per hour, which makes for a peaceful morning activity along the Portuguese quarter or Devka promenade. Gearless scooters (Activas) are available across Nani Daman for around ₹400–₹600/day.
  • Auto Rickshaws: Abundant and ideal for quick family hops between Nani Daman, Moti Daman, and the beach strips without moving your car from hotel parking.
  • 🚨 Avoid Electric AC Buses: The local electric AC buses have erratic schedules. Waiting for them wastes valuable holiday time; stick to your own vehicle or autos.

Essential Travel Details

  • Best Time to Visit: October to March offers mild coastal breezes and pleasant evenings. Monsoon (July–September) is lush and scenic for driving, but beach water sports will be paused.
  • Driving Time:
    • From Mumbai: Approximately 3.5 to 4 hours via NH48.
    • From Pune: Approximately 5.5 to 6.5 hours via Mumbai-Pune Expressway and NH48.
