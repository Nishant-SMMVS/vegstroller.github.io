E3 - 16 Smart Tips For Flight Bookings | Europe Trip Planning

Written during our Europe trip planning phase in April 2023. Prices and rules may have changed since, so please check before you book.

In the previous episodes, we settled the debate between Package Tours and Self-Planned trips and discussed how to outline your perfect itinerary. Now that you have zeroed in on the cities you want to cover, it is time to lock in your flights.

Booking flights to Europe isn't just about searching a date and clicking 'buy'. If you aren't prepared, you will end up delaying the process, facing payment rejections, or paying inflated prices. To prevent that, I have compiled 16 smart tips you must know before booking your international flights.

For your convenience, I have divided these tips into three chapters: Credit Card Hacks, Booking Preparations, and Flight Selection.

Table of Contents

Chapter 1: Credit Card Hacks for Maximum Savings

  1. Choose the right credit card: Base your choice on the Reward Return Ratio to maximize benefits and effectively lower your overall ticket price.
  2. Time your booking with card offers: Travel websites like MakeMyTrip, Yatra, and Cleartrip run daily discounts tied to specific banks. Find out which day offers the best discount for your specific card and book then.
  3. Read the fine print: Understand the minimum transaction value and the maximum discount cap. Check whether the offer is a flat discount or a percentage.
  4. Enable online and international spends: Do this before you sit down to book. Many transactions fail simply because international usage is toggled off on the banking app.
  5. Increase your transaction limits: Ensure your online spend limit is well above the ticket price to avoid last-minute rejections.
  6. Redeem existing reward points: Don't hoard them! Use them to get heavily discounted or even free flights.

Chapter 2: Booking Preparations

  1. Use trustworthy websites: Do not fall prey to phishing or fake websites just because they offer flights at slightly cheaper prices. Stick to reputed aggregators or direct airline sites.
  2. Use local currency for internal flights: If you are booking intra-Europe flights, use the local country website and make payments in the local currency to save money on forex conversions (assuming you use a zero-markup forex card).
  3. Keep all passenger details handy: Have a document ready with passport numbers, expiry dates, dates and places of issue, DOBs, full names as per passport, meal preferences, and frequent flyer numbers.
  4. Pre-add passenger details: Add these details into your travel website account beforehand to speed up the booking process and avoid typos in a rush.
  5. Do not repeatedly check prices: Book your tickets as early as possible, but do not repeatedly search the same route. Browser cookies track your searches, which can trigger sudden price increases.

Chapter 3: Flight Selection Strategy

  1. Prefer the same airline for connections: If your trip involves a connecting flight, sticking to one airline ensures your luggage is transferred seamlessly to your final destination.
  2. Keep a long layover for mixed airlines: If you absolutely must book connecting flights on different airlines, leave a minimum layover of 4 hours to account for delays, immigration, and baggage transfers.
  3. Time your arrival: Prefer airlines that reach Europe in the late afternoon or early evening. This helps you sleep at a normal local time and significantly reduces jetlag.
  4. Compare multi-city vs. round-trip: Always check the price difference between arriving and departing from the same city versus arriving in one city and flying out from another (multi-city). Multi-city flights save you a wasted day of backtracking!
  5. Always book refundable flights: If you do not have your Schengen Visa approved yet, never book a non-refundable flight. The slight premium for a refundable ticket is worth the absolute peace of mind.
