Planned by a Family Like Yours

Finding a luxury hotel is easy. Planning a trip that works for the whole family is not. Kids need space, a pool and days that aren't rushed. Grandparents, when they come along, need comfortable rooms and shorter walks. And everyone needs proper vegetarian food. So we write down everything we plan for our own trips: the stays that worked for our family, day-by-day itineraries paced for kids and seniors, and the vegetarian food we found at each stop. If it doesn't work for a family like ours, it doesn't make our list.

Me, at Lauterbrunnen, Switzerland

Who we are

Hi, we're Nishant and family

We're a Pune family of four: me, my wife and our two daughters. Our first big trip together was Thailand in January 2020, just before the COVID lockdown. Since then I've written up every trip we take.

That's 26 days across Europe that I planned myself, a week in Ladakh, Singapore, Kashmir, and over 15 shorter trips across Rishikesh, Goa, Gujarat, Kerala, Maharashtra and many other places. I do all the planning, research and booking, and everything I learn ends up here and on our Instagram and YouTube.

We're not a travel agency. We don't sell packages or take bookings. We just share what worked for our family, and what didn't.
