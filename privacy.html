Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

VegStroller is a family travel website run by Nishant Gupta from Pune, India. This page explains, in plain words, what information the site collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Anonymous usage data. We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are read and how visitors find us (for example, country, device and pages visited). It does not tell us who you are. Google Analytics uses cookies; you can read how Google uses this data, and you can block these cookies in your browser settings.
  • Emails you send us. If you write to vegstroller@gmail.com, we keep your email only to reply to you. We never add you to a mailing list without asking.

We do not ask for, store or sell personal details such as your phone number, address or payment information. The site has no accounts or log-ins.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site, such as links to check hotel rates, may be affiliate links. If you click one and make a booking, the booking site may pay us a small commission, at no extra cost to you. When you click such a link, the booking site or its affiliate network may set its own cookies to track the referral; their own privacy policies apply to anything you do on their sites.

Our hotel reviews and recommendations are our own, based on our stays and research, and are never changed because of a commission.

Links to other sites

We link to Instagram, YouTube, hotels and other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Questions or requests

To ask a question about this policy, write to vegstroller@gmail.com. We may update this page from time to time; the date at the top shows the latest version.
